A Warm Welcome to our Local History Website …

Created in 2012, we are dedicated to celebrating and sustaining the local history of our beautiful Cheshire village which lies south east of Chester and in promoting community-wide engagement and inclusion.

Oddfellows’ Procession in centre of Tattenhall

Our core principles:

  • to provide a lasting legacy, committed to promoting and showcasing the heritage of Tattenhall and its immediate locality
  • to provide a platform for raising awareness and curiosity about our local heritage through the collection and preservation of archival material

Our site is very easy to navigate using the menu on the toolbar at the top of the page and then clicking on any of the links.

We are immensely grateful to all those individuals who have contributed materials for use on the site.

We have tried to contact the owners of copyright of any materials we have used and we have tried to ensure that all the material on the site is factually accurate.

The inclusion of a link to another webpage or website does not imply any recommendation on our part and we are not responsible for the content of any information accessed through these links.

  • ‘LATEST NEWS’

    If you’re a newcomer to the village of Tattenhall then this is a great place to whet your appetite for a real sense of place and of times past.

    Take a quick look under the ‘History’ section on the Toolbar before looking at individual topics, all of which are clearly labelled – just double click on any heading to be found on the Toolbar.

    Our book, ‘Tattenhall – A Community United by War‘, is now available, donations from which support The Royal British Legion and domain costs in hosting the Website.

    The book covers both conflicts and seeks to raise awareness about the individual war experiences of many local men but also explores the challenges faced by the women and men who remained at home. Much of the content is new and complements information which is already to be found on this Website.

    If you would like a copy then please contact – terri9hull@gmail.com

    Our circular walk ‘Historical Trail Pamphlet’ can be downloaded from the site (to be found under ‘History’).

    Our ‘Historical Interpretation Panel’ is also located opposite the War Memorial.

    An ‘Artefacts Display Cabinet’ is to be found in the stairwell at the Barbour Institute.

    More substantial and privately owned artefacts have been photographed and are listed in the ‘Gallery’ on the Toolbar.

    And finally, if you have any artefacts/photographs that you feel might be useful to include on the Website, then do get in touch.
